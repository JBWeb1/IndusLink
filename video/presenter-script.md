# Nexus Accounting: presenter video script

**Length:** about 90 seconds (roughly 240 spoken words at a relaxed pace)
**Format:** one presenter talking to camera, with cutaways to Nexus screens
**Set:** a desk with a laptop, a phone and a shoebox overflowing with receipts

Replace anything in [square brackets] before filming.

---

## 1. Hook (0:00–0:08)

**Shot:** medium close-up. Presenter at the desk, night-time lighting, the receipt box in frame.

> If you run a small business, you know this feeling. It's ten at night, the receipts are in a shoebox, the invoices are somewhere in your email, and VAT is due on Friday.

**On screen:** small text pops up beside the presenter: "VAT due: Friday".

## 2. Introduction (0:08–0:16)

**Shot:** presenter pushes the shoebox aside and turns square to camera.

> I'm [your name], and I built Nexus Accounting so that never has to happen again. It puts all your business finances in one place and does most of the boring work for you.

**On screen:** lower third: "[Your name], Founder, Nexus Accounting". Nexus logo in the corner.

## 3. Invoicing (0:16–0:30)

**Shot:** presenter for the first line, then cut to the invoice screen.

> Let's start with invoicing. You can create a professional invoice in under a minute, send it by email or WhatsApp, and see the moment your customer pays.

**Cutaway:** an invoice being filled in, the Send button, then the status changing from "Sent" to "Paid".

## 4. Bank matching (0:30–0:44)

**Shot:** cutaway, then back to presenter for the last sentence.

> Nexus brings in your bank transactions and matches them to your invoices and bills. So instead of losing a weekend to reconciling, you check the matches and confirm.

**Cutaway:** a list of bank transactions, each getting a green "Matched" tick.

## 5. Receipts (0:44–0:56)

**Shot:** close-up of the presenter's hands photographing a real receipt with the phone.

> Got a receipt? Take a photo. Nexus reads the supplier, the amount and the VAT, and files it for you. The shoebox can finally retire.

**Cutaway:** the phone screen with the receipt and the fields filling in: supplier, date, amount, VAT. Optional gag: the presenter drops the shoebox in the bin.

## 6. VAT (0:56–1:08)

**Shot:** presenter, then cutaway.

> When your VAT period ends, your return is already worked out, and every number links back to the invoice or receipt behind it. So if anyone asks where a figure came from, you can show them in two clicks.

**Cutaway:** VAT summary (output VAT, input VAT, amount payable). Click one figure and it opens the invoice behind it.

## 7. Dashboard (1:08–1:18)

**Shot:** cutaway to the dashboard, then back to the presenter, now in daylight.

> And every morning, one dashboard shows you what matters: cash in the bank, who owes you money, and what's due next.

**Cutaway:** dashboard with the bank balance, the "Owed to you" total and a short list of upcoming bills.

## 8. Close (1:18–1:30)

**Shot:** presenter at a clear, tidy desk in daylight. Relaxed and smiling.

> Nexus Accounting. Less admin, more business. Try it free at [your website], and get your evenings back.

**On screen:** Nexus logo, "[your website]" and a "Start your free trial" button. Hold for 3 seconds after the last word.

## 30-second version for social media

Use scenes 1, 5 and 8 with this middle line:

> Invoices, bank matching, receipts and VAT, all in one place. Nexus does the boring work so you don't have to.

---

## What you need

**Props:** laptop, phone, shoebox of real-looking receipts, a bin (for the optional gag).

**Screens to record or mock up:**
1. Invoice being created, sent, then marked paid
2. Bank transactions getting matched
3. Phone receipt capture with fields filling in
4. VAT summary with a figure that opens its invoice
5. Dashboard
6. Logo end card with website and free-trial button

**Filming tips:**
- Film each section as a separate take. It's far easier than one 90-second take, and the cutaways hide the joins.
- Use a teleprompter app on a phone or tablet just below the lens so your eyes stay near the camera.
- Film scenes 1 and 2 in evening light and scene 8 in daylight. The before-and-after is the story.
- Read the lines out loud before filming and change any wording that doesn't sound like you.

## Check before you publish

- **Claims:** only keep features and promises the product really delivers, such as "under a minute", WhatsApp sending, bank feeds, reading receipts and a free trial. Cut or soften any that aren't true yet.
- **Name:** other companies use "Nexus" in their names. Check the trademark register and company registry in your country before you spend money on the brand.
